HabitSolo Privacy Policy

Build habits. Grow your garden.

Summary

HabitSolo works completely offline. We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. All your habits and progress stay on your device.

Effective date: October 7, 2026  |  App: HabitSolo  |  Package: com.pixelandcode.habitsolo

1. Who we are

HabitSolo is a habit tracking app developed by Pixel and Code ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no account, no sign in and no registration.

The following information is created by you inside the app and is saved only on your device:

  • The name you enter for the greeting on the home screen
  • Habit names, icons and repeat settings
  • Your check-ins, streaks and progress
  • Your theme choice (light, dark or system)

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Internet access and permissions

  • The app does not use the internet and does not connect to any server.
  • The app does not request any Android permissions, such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or notifications.

4. Third-party services

HabitSolo does not contain advertising, analytics, crash reporting, tracking tools or any other third-party SDKs. No data is shared with third parties.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in the private storage area of the app on your device and is protected by the Android system. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot access, recover or delete it for you.

If you have turned on Android device backup in your phone settings, your device or Google account may include app data in that backup. This is controlled by your device settings and by Google, not by HabitSolo.

6. Your control over your data

  • You can edit or delete any habit inside the app at any time.
  • You can remove all app data by clearing the app storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

7. Children's privacy

HabitSolo does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since no data leaves the device, no personal data of children is collected or shared.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. The updated version will be published at this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
